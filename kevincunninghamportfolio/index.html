About

Kevin Cunningham

“Take time from creating to think, research, then refine and build.”

Art Center is me. B.S. in Transportation Design, 1994 — and thirty years since, split evenly: 15 years in vehicles, 15 years in art direction and video games.

Motorcycles at Honda R&D. VP of Design twice over — Hiero Tech, where the Samsung STAR UGV went from concept to a working program with partner Rickey Newmayer, and Land Systems, on autonomous ground vehicles. Toy design end-to-end at Lanard — concept through final on-shelf sample — and concept work for Hasbro. Lead artist at Activision, senior artist at Savage Entertainment (James Bond, Star Wars, Transformers), freelance concept design for Sega of America, and Chief Creative Director at Studio Uchuu.

Building digitally and by hand is a must for the designer — its importance can’t be overstated. The computer keeps it precise and efficient.
